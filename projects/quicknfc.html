QuickNFC

An Android application developed for a dedicated NFC reader device, focused on device-connected reading workflows.

AndroidNFCDevice integrationIdentity-reading workflows

My role & ownership

I independently developed QuickNFC as an Android application for an NFC reader device. The project focuses on device-connected reading workflows rather than a cross-platform consumer mobile app.

Project context

Identity and access workflows often require mobile software to communicate with physical readers and respond clearly to different device states. QuickNFC represents hands-on work in this hardware-adjacent space.

Areas of work

  • Android application workflows for identity reading
  • NFC and device-connected interactions
  • User interface states around scanning and feedback
  • Integration-oriented debugging and validation

Engineering approach

The work centers on connecting Android UI behavior with device capabilities. In this class of application, predictable feedback, error handling and repeatable tests matter as much as the interface itself.

Engineering considerations

  • Device availability and reader permissions
  • Clear feedback when a scan cannot complete
  • Keeping the UI understandable during hardware interactions
